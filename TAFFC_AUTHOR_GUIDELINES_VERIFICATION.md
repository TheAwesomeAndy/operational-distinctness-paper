## 2. Page limit / overlength policy
- **Free limit: 12 formatted pages** for Transactions regular papers, **including references and
  author biographies**.
- **Overlength: $200 per page** (Mandatory Overlength Page Charges), assessed after final layout
  for any page or fraction beyond 12.
- **Hard maximum: 18 formatted pages** for a regular-paper submission.
- **Implication for ARSPI-Net:** the manuscript is **10 formatted pages**, so it is **within the
  free 12-page limit with no overlength charge**. No compression or TAFFC overlay is required;
  the existing journal-neutral `manuscript/main.pdf` is reused unchanged.
- *Source:* IEEE Computer Society "Mandatory Overlength Page Charges – Effective 18 Feb. 2018,"
  which **explicitly lists IEEE Transactions on Affective Computing** among the covered journals.

## 3. Supplemental material allowed?
**Yes**, and explicitly encouraged: the page-charge policy states that "supporting but nonessential
information should be submitted as supplemental material." The existing 6-page `supplement.pdf`
is uploaded as supplemental material.
